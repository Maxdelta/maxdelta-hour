# Timeline — Mundy Township / Genesee County Megasite

## Late 2021
Michigan launches the SOAR-era strategy for large industrial attraction deals. More legislative sourcing should be added here.

## Early 2022
Initial state support begins for site preparation tied to the Flint/Genesee megasite effort, according to later Bridge Michigan reporting.

## August 2022
Flint/Genesee site efforts reportedly share in a $5 million state award, according to Bridge Michigan.

## April 2023
Michigan Strategic Fund reportedly awards another $1.5 million connected to Flint and Genesee Chamber Foundation / Flint & Genesee Group.

## 2023
Mundy Township adds an Advanced Manufacturing Overlay District. Bridge Michigan reported township officials were under nondisclosure agreements and that the megasite connection was not part of the public discussion.

## April 2024
MEDC references $9.2 million in SSRP support before the larger May 2024 approval.

## May 21, 2024
Michigan Strategic Fund approves $250 million in SSRP funds for the Flint and Genesee Group Foundation. MEDC says the money will support land assembly and infrastructure development for the Advanced Manufacturing District.

## June 2025
Bridge Michigan reports Michigan has invested at least $260 million toward buying land southwest of Flint, about 165 homes are targeted for demolition, and Gov. Whitmer is publicly pushing to land a semiconductor plant.

## July 2025
SanDisk withdraws from the project. Reporting cites national economic turmoil / federal uncertainty as the reason given by state officials. Sen. John Cherry publicly identifies SanDisk and calls the decision disappointing.

## August 2025
Residents testify before lawmakers about the project’s impact. Bridge Michigan reports secrecy concerns, NDA concerns, and that the site remains on the market.

## November 2025
Bridge Michigan reports megasite developers received $261.25 million from MEDC/MSF, including $194 million to Flint and Genesee Group Foundation for site assembly and preparation.

## Current episode framing
The story is no longer just “Did Michigan land the factory?” It is now:

1. What did taxpayers fund?
2. Who benefited from the site preparation?
3. What happens to residents when a speculative megasite moves forward?
4. Should major incentive deals require more public transparency before money is committed?

## Notes

Some reported investment totals and projected job numbers vary between sources depending on when they were published and whether they refer to permanent jobs, construction jobs, or total economic impact. Where possible, this episode references the original source for each figure rather than presenting a single definitive number.
