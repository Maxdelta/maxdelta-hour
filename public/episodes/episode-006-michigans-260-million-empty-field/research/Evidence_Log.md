# Evidence Standard

Every factual statement used in the podcast should trace back to at least one source listed below. Where multiple reputable sources report different figures, the script should acknowledge the range or attribute the specific figure to its source rather than presenting a disputed number as settled fact.

Evidence status definitions:

- **Strong** – Supported by primary sources or multiple independent high-quality sources.
- **Medium-Strong** – Supported by reputable reporting but with some variation in reported figures.
- **Medium** – Supported by limited reporting or requires additional verification before being presented as definitive.

## Wording Guidance
Avoid saying “Michigan wasted $260 million” as a factual claim. Safer wording:

> Michigan has committed/spent roughly $260 million preparing the site, and after SanDisk withdrew, taxpayers are left asking whether the risk was justified.

Avoid saying “empty field” literally if site preparation/demolition is ongoing. Use it as a title/metaphor, then clarify:

> It is not literally untouched land. It is land assembled and prepared at taxpayer expense without the promised anchor project.
