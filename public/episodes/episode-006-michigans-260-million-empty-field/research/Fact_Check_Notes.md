# Fact Check Notes

## Claims safe to use now
- MEDC/MSF approved $250M in SSRP funds in May 2024.
- The $250M followed $9.2M in earlier SSRP support.
- Reporting later places total awards around $261.25M.
- The site is in Mundy Township / Genesee County near Flint Bishop Airport.
- SanDisk withdrew from the project in July 2025.
- The project was reported as a massive semiconductor deal with roughly 9,400-10,000 jobs promised.

## Claims needing careful wording
- “Spent” vs “approved,” “awarded,” or “committed.” Use “approved/awarded” unless source specifically says spent.
- “$63B” vs “$55B.” Use range or say “reported as high as $63B.”
- “Empty field.” Use as title/metaphor. Clarify site is assembled/prepared land, not necessarily untouched land.
- “Secret deal.” Better wording: “secretive process,” “NDAs,” “limited public details,” unless discussing specific confirmed NDA documents.

## Claims to avoid unless stronger sourcing is added
- Direct accusations of corruption.
- Claims that specific officials knowingly misled the public.
- Claims that all $261.25M is unrecoverable.
- Claims that every home has been demolished.

## Episode Standard

When evidence is mixed or evolving:

- Attribute the claim to its source.
- Prefer ranges over single disputed figures.
- Distinguish between fact, reporting, and analysis.
- Avoid stating motive unless directly supported by evidence.
