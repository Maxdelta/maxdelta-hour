# Research Sources — Episode 006

## Research Standard

This episode prioritizes primary government documents, official statements, and high-quality reporting. When multiple reputable sources report different figures or timelines, the original source is cited and the difference is acknowledged rather than presenting a single unsupported number.

Sources are organized by confidence level:

- Tier 1 — Primary / Official Sources
- Tier 2 — High-Quality Reporting
- Tier 3 — Perspective / Advocacy

## Tier 1 — Primary / Official Sources

### Michigan Economic Development Corporation / Michigan Business
**Funding for Site Readiness Efforts, Support for Community Revitalization to Bring New Jobs and Investment to Michigan**  
URL: https://www.michiganbusiness.org/press-releases/2024/05/msf/

Key use:
- Confirms $250 million in SSRP funds approved.
- Confirms this built on $9.2 million in prior SSRP support.
- States the money was intended to help Flint and Genesee Group Foundation attract investment through land assembly and infrastructure development.

Quote note:
- Use sparingly. Main citation supports the funding approval and official rationale.

### Advanced Manufacturing District of Genesee County
URL: https://developflintandgenesee.org/advanced-manufacturing-district-of-genesee-county/

## Tier 2 — High-Quality Reporting

### Bridge Michigan — July 16, 2025
**Michigan loses massive semiconductor plant. Whitmer blames ‘national economic turmoil’**  
URL: https://bridgemi.com/business-watch/michigan-loses-massive-semiconductor-plant-whitmer-blames-national-economic-turmoil/

Key use:
- Reports a $63 billion semiconductor complex.
- Reports 10,000 promised jobs.
- Reports roughly $260 million in taxpayer funding spent preparing a 1,300-acre megasite.
- Reports Gov. Whitmer’s explanation: national economic turmoil.

### Bridge Michigan — June 6, 2025
**Whitmer’s semiconductor dreams near Flint become nightmare for neighbors**  
URL: https://bridgemi.com/business-watch/whitmers-semiconductor-dreams-near-flint-become-nightmare-neighbors/

Key use:
- Reports state investment of at least $260 million toward land acquisition southwest of Flint.
- Reports about 165 homes targeted for demolition.
- Useful for resident impact and secrecy concerns.

### Bridge Michigan — August 20, 2025
**Residents call Michigan’s $261M Flint-area megasite development ‘traumatic’**  
URL: https://bridgemi.com/business-watch/residents-call-michigans-271m-flint-area-megasite-development-traumatic/

Key use:
- Reports 1,300-acre site south of Flint Bishop Airport.
- Reports Sandisk as leading contender and 9,400 jobs.
- Reports nondisclosure agreements and resident testimony.
- Reports alleged $6.2 billion total subsidy package and $660,000 per job estimate based on published figures.

### Bridge Michigan — November 11, 2025
**Michigan has spent $261M on Mundy megasite. Now, the public weighs in**  
URL: https://bridgemi.com/business-watch/michigan-has-spent-261m-on-mundy-megasite-now-the-public-weighs/

Key use:
- Reports megasite developers received $261.25 million from MEDC/MSF.
- Reports $194 million went to Flint and Genesee Group Foundation for site assembly and preparation.
- Reports work included purchasing/demolishing homes, removing trees, and removing drainage culverts.

### Michigan Advance / News From The States — July 16, 2025
**Lawmakers respond as Sandisk pulls out of Mundy Township semiconductor project**  
URL: https://www.newsfromthestates.com/article/lawmakers-respond-sandisk-pulls-out-mundy-township-semiconductor-project

Key use:
- Reports company dropped the effort citing “massive uncertainty” at the federal level.
- Reports Whitmer statement that company decided against building a semiconductor plant anywhere in the U.S.
- Identifies John Cherry naming SanDisk.
